## Shared direction

Use case ui-mockup. Existing Vietnamese browser enterprise game Đi Tắt Đón Đầu. Premium handcrafted board-game interface: burgundy lacquer, delicate engraved double-gold frames, cream parchment, orange-red embossed buttons, hand-painted industrial/worker/event illustrations, readable Vietnamese serif lettering. No photorealism, modern SaaS restyle, neon, device mockups or unrelated menus. Local motion only, clear before/during/after, confirmed model data, integer people/clusters/products, no blocking toasts or new acknowledgement. Static approval art, not implementation.
