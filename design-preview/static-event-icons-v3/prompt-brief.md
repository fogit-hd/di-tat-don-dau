## Shared art direction

Redesign the supplied sheets as a NEW STATIC ICON APPROVAL CANVAS for Đi Tắt Đón Đầu. Inputs are STYLE REFERENCES: preserve the approved rich hand-painted gouache/watercolor game-item quality, fine irregular dark ink edges, substantial three-quarter volume, highly detailed metal/wood/fabric, warm highlights and natural jewel colors. Absolutely no flat vector/SVG pictograms, no background photographs, no pasted small factory scene, no collage, no white paper rectangles behind icons, no generic corner badges or arrows decorating the images. Each icon is one cohesive fully painted object illustration with complete silhouette and ample empty margins. This is PREVIEW ONLY, not production UI. Wide landscape, high resolution, 4 columns by 3 rows, 12 large objects on warm pale parchment #fff1d6, narrow wine-red header with cream Vietnamese serif title, short burgundy serif labels centered under each object. Header subtitle exactly 'Ảnh tĩnh · Bản duyệt'. Keep all labels readable and accurately accented. No invented stats, no extra icons, no animation, no GIF.
