# Production art prompts / provenance

All generated with built-in ImageGen, transparent_background enabled for sprite assets. No CLI/API fallback. Output IDs and final dimensions are in `frontend/public/assets/living-hero-v1/manifest.json`. Runtime art is saved in the project; original PNGs stay in ImageGen storage.

1. Desktop clean plate: use the accepted desktop canvas as visual reference; preserve hand-painted vintage Vietnamese brick factory, conveyor opening, fixed crane tower/boom, mountains, harbor, paved foreground road, orange orchard and crates. Left ~40% is blank pale parchment for the existing DOM title/form. Remove UI, lettering, foreground paired workers, moving truck, nearest boat, crane cable/load, belt parcels and chimney smoke so these can be separate moving layers. Stable daylight, no photorealistic treatment, no extra objects or baked text. Selected `188fd090-df97-4610-b8af-be222c4dc727`.
2. Portrait clean plate: same production art family and remove the same moving objects, recompose the factory left / crane upper right / water right / foreground lane and orchard for a complete portrait scene; no UI, no cropping actors out of a desktop screenshot. Selected `14a049b7-d4d4-4f0b-aeae-4151a89897ca`.
3. Workers: transparent 4×2 whole-character pose sheet, same pair of Vietnamese dock workers handling one wooden crate. Left navy cap, cream sleeves/navy overalls; right conical hat, cream/navy trousers. Complete feet, elbows and heads inside each cell. Sequential bend/lower/lift/rest poses; same faces, clothes, scale, camera, light and foot baseline. No dismembered body rig, cell borders, background or labels. Selected `36bbde5a-1b0f-4a74-8fbf-5818f427914a`.
4. Mixed props atlas: 3×2 transparent vintage truck, cargo boat, hooked crate, plain wooden parcel, smoke and painted water flecks, in the reference scene's watercolor/ink style. The upper row objects crossed cell boundaries, so those vehicles/hoist were rejected. The independent lower crate and water cells are retained. Source `127efa11-65b4-470f-ab9d-d4884e31a508`.
5. Truck replacement: one complete red vintage delivery truck with green covered wooden cargo bed, facing right, clean alpha, no ground/background/extra objects/contact shadow; detailed painted wheels and matching factory-scene perspective/daylight. Selected `b4d6dbac-024d-4fa8-bff7-a1f4c5362aca`.
6. Boat replacement: one complete small dark-blue wooden cargo boat facing left, wooden wheelhouse and stacked wooden parcels, no crew/water/background/extra objects, true transparency, hand-painted vintage harbor style. Selected `94cddade-9579-4463-97f1-e796e9b907db`.
7. Hoist replacement: one complete wooden cargo crate hanging from short triangular ropes and a black hook at top center. Entire hook/ropes/crate visible, matching hand-painted harbor style, isolated transparent background with no other objects. Selected `a1a3114c-b6fa-4a6a-baf7-a78dce16281c`.
8. Smoke replacement (full prompt): “Create a production transparent 2D game VFX sprite, isolated hand-painted watercolor and colored-pencil steam puff, warm ivory highlights and slate gray-blue shaded underside, soft ragged painted edges. Single small organic elongated cloud drifting diagonally up-left, tapering to a narrow bottom-right tail that can emerge from a brick factory chimney. Vintage Vietnamese industrial harbor board-game illustration, sunlight from upper left, textured paint. Real alpha transparency everywhere outside the puff, no background, no ground, no rectangular haze, no glow, no text, no contact shadow. Centered with generous transparent margins. This is a clean overlay sprite for a looping chimney smoke animation.” Selected `e83f328e-c24d-4669-a948-145806b39e71`.

The first seven briefs are condensed records of the prompts used during the asset pass. Assets are packed/encoded by `scripts/prepare-living-hero.py`; no creative art redraw is performed by the script.
